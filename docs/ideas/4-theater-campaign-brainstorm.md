# Brainstorm: 4-theater, real-battle campaign structure

**Status: not in scope. Do not implement. Do not treat as design intent.**

This file is a raw brainstorm, generated 2026-09-04, and nothing more. It is
kept for reference in case a future, deliberate redesign wants a starting
point — it is not a proposal in progress and nobody has signed off on it.

## Why this conflicts with the current GDD

This structure directly contradicts two decisions in `docs/GDD.md` that are
currently in force:

- **§9.2 — one 12-mission campaign spine.** The GDD calls this "the single
  most important scope decision in the document": one 12-mission structure
  (3 acts × 4 missions, 8 maps) reused by both alliances via briefing-text
  mirroring, not per-nation or per-theater campaigns. The list below is 4
  campaigns × 10 missions — roughly 3.3× that scope.
- **§14.2 / §14.3 — fictionalized, non-historical framing.** The GDD requires
  invented theaters, invented operation names, and briefings that avoid
  dates, real place names, and real commanders. This is load-bearing, not
  decorative: it's what lets any nation play any map without a false
  historical claim, avoids implying the game takes a position on real
  events, and is specifically designed to clear content review (including
  German market requirements) without a separate build. The list below is
  the opposite of that: real, named, dated historical battles.

Using this list would require a deliberate GDD revision (updating §9.2 and
rewriting §14.2/§14.3's rationale, not just swapping mission names) and a
`docs/DECISIONS.md` entry explaining the change — not a quiet drop-in. Until
that happens, `docs/GDD.md` remains authoritative and this file is inert.

## The brainstorm

Four campaigns, one per theater, 10 missions each, land battles only,
running chronologically through that theater's real WWII timeline and
escalating from early/smaller engagements to late-war, heavily fortified
battles. Titles only — no mission design, balance, or map work implied.

### Campaign 1: Western Front (1940–1945)
1. Battle of Sedan
2. Battle of the Ardennes Forest
3. Battle of Dunkirk
4. Battle of Caen
5. Battle of the Hedgerows (Operation Cobra)
6. Battle of the Falaise Pocket
7. Battle of Arnhem (Operation Market Garden)
8. Battle of the Bulge
9. Battle of the Reichswald (Rhineland)
10. Battle of the Ruhr Pocket

### Campaign 2: Eastern Front (1941–1945)
1. Battle of Brody
2. Battle of Smolensk
3. Battle of Kiev
4. Battle of Moscow
5. Battle of Stalingrad
6. Battle of Kursk
7. Battle of the Dnieper
8. Operation Bagration (Belarus)
9. Vistula–Oder Offensive
10. Battle of Berlin

### Campaign 3: Mediterranean & Italy (1940–1945)
1. Battle of Sidi Barrani
2. Siege of Tobruk
3. Battle of Gazala
4. First Battle of El Alamein
5. Second Battle of El Alamein
6. Battle of Kasserine Pass
7. Battle of Sicily (Operation Husky)
8. Battle of Monte Cassino
9. Battle of Anzio
10. Battle of the Gothic Line (Bologna)

### Campaign 4: Pacific Theater (1941–1945)
1. Battle of Malaya
2. Battle of Bataan
3. Battle of Guadalcanal
4. Battle of Buna–Gona
5. Battle of Tarawa
6. Battle of Kohima–Imphal
7. Battle of Saipan
8. Battle of Peleliu
9. Battle of Iwo Jima
10. Battle of Okinawa
