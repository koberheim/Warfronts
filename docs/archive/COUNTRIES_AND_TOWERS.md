# WW2 Tower Defense - Countries & Tower Reference

This document outlines all playable countries and their unique tower types. In the full game, players will select a country at the start of each match, and all towers will be themed around that nation's military forces.

---

## 🇺🇸 United States (Allies)

**Theme:** Industrial powerhouse, mass production, combined arms warfare

### Tower Types

1. **Browning MG Nest** - Heavy machine gun emplacement
2. **Sherman Tank Emplacement** - Mobile tank turret
3. **Bazooka Squad** - Anti-tank infantry
4. **Ranger Sniper Post** - Long-range precision fire
5. **Jeep Recon Tower** - Fast attack vehicle
6. **Mustang Air Support Beacon** - Air strike support
7. **105mm Howitzer Battery** - Heavy artillery
8. **M3 Halftrack Turret** - Mobile weapons platform
9. **Naval Gunfire Spotter** - Calls in offshore bombardment
10. **War Bonds Supply Depot** - Economy/support building

### 🌟 Unique Tower: "Arsenal of Democracy Factory"

**Mechanic:** Production tower that periodically spawns temporary AI-controlled units (jeeps, infantry squads, or mini Shermans) that drive along the path attacking enemies.

**Playstyle:** Instead of pure DPS, overwhelms enemies with reinforcements, reflecting US industrial power and mass production capability.

**Strengths:**
- Creates mobile units that can attack multiple points
- Scales well with longer paths
- Good against scattered enemies

**Weaknesses:**
- Units are temporary and don't persist
- Less effective on short paths
- Requires time to build up unit production

## 🇬🇧 Britain (Allies)

**Theme:** Defensive resilience, air superiority, tactical precision

### Tower Types

1. **Vickers Machine Gun Nest** - Reliable suppression fire
2. **Churchill Tank Bunker** - Heavy armor emplacement
3. **Spitfire Air Command** - Fighter plane support
4. **Royal Artillery Battery** - Long-range bombardment
5. **SAS Ambush Post** - Special operations unit
6. **Bofors AA Platform** - Anti-air defense
7. **Mortar Pit** - Indirect fire support
8. **Bren Gun Squad** - Mobile infantry
9. **Radar Early Warning Tower** - Detection and support
10. **Royal Engineers Minefield** - Area denial

### 🌟 Unique Tower: "RAF Scramble Command"

**Mechanic:** When enemies reach a certain point on the map, launches fighter planes that strafe the entire path in a line, damaging everything caught in the attack.

**Playstyle:** Cooldown-based ability with large burst damage representing rapid RAF interception tactics.

**Strengths:**
- Massive burst damage potential
- Can clear entire sections of path
- Great against clustered enemies

**Weaknesses:**
- Long cooldown between attacks
- Requires enemies to trigger threshold
- Predictable attack pattern

**Theme:** Overwhelming firepower, sacrifice, brutal efficiency

### Tower Types

1. **Maxim MG Bunker** - Sustained machine gun fire
2. **Katyusha Rocket Truck** - Multiple rocket launcher
3. **T-34 Defensive Turret** - Main battle tank
4. **Red Army Mortar Squad** - Infantry support
5. **Anti-Tank Rifle Team** - Armor penetration
6. **Commissar Command Post** - Morale/buff tower
7. **Field Artillery Battery** - Heavy guns
8. **Siberian Sniper Nest** - Elite marksmen
9. **Minefield Layer** - Explosive traps
10. **Armored Train Cannon** - Railway artillery

### 🌟 Unique Tower: "Katyusha Storm Battery"

**Mechanic:** Charges up over time. When fully charged, unleashes a massive rocket barrage covering multiple sections of the path simultaneously, dealing huge area damage but requiring a long recharge.

**Playstyle:** High-risk, high-reward timing-based gameplay.

**Strengths:**
- Devastating area damage
- Can hit multiple path sections
- Game-changing when timed correctly

**Weaknesses:**
- Very long recharge time
- Useless while charging
- Must time activation carefully

### Tower Types

1. **MG42 Bunker** - Extremely fast-firing machine gun
2. **Panzer IV Turret** - Medium tank emplacement
3. **Tiger Tank Platform** - Heavy armor powerhouse
4. **Nebelwerfer Rocket Battery** - Rocket artillery
5. **Wehrmacht Mortar Team** - Infantry support
6. **Flak 88 Cannon** - Dual-purpose anti-tank/anti-air
7. **Stuka Dive-Bomb Beacon** - Precision air strike
8. **Pak 40 Anti-Tank Gun** - Dedicated AT weapon
9. **Sturmgrenadier Post** - Assault infantry
10. **Radar Flak Tower** - Detection and AA

### 🌟 Unique Tower: "Blitzkrieg Command Post"

**Mechanic:** Periodically activates "Blitz Mode" for nearby towers - increases attack speed and projectile velocity dramatically for a short burst.

**Playstyle:** Encourages clustered defenses and aggressive timing windows.

**Strengths:**
- Massive DPS spike when activated
- Benefits all nearby towers
- Creates strong defensive positions

**Weaknesses:**
- Requires towers to be grouped
- Downtime between activations
- Loses value if poorly positioned

3. **Alpini Mountain Rifle Post** - Elite mountain troops

10. **Naval Patrol Cannon** - Ship-based artillery

### 🌟 Unique Tower: "Bersaglieri Charge Post"

**Mechanic:** Spawns fast-moving infantry that sprint ahead of enemies, throwing grenades and temporarily slowing enemy formations.

**Playstyle:** Tactical harassment tower rather than pure damage - disrupts enemy movement and creates opportunities.

**Strengths:**
- Slows and disrupts enemy formations

- Good for buying time

**Weaknesses:**

- Units can be killed

## 🇯🇵 Japan (Axis)

**Theme:** Fanatical defense, honor, kamikaze tactics

### Tower Types

1. **Type 92 Machine Gun Nest** - Standard MG
2. **Imperial Mortar Squad** - Infantry support
3. **Type 97 Chi-Ha Turret** - Medium tank
4. **Sniper Hideout** - Precision elimination
5. **Bamboo Spike Trap** - Defensive obstacles
6. **Coastal Naval Gun** - Ship artillery
7. **Banzai Infantry Post** - Suicide charge unit
8. **Anti-Tank Minefield** - AT traps
9. **Artillery Observation Tower** - Spotting/support
10. **Zero Fighter Beacon** - Air superiority

### 🌟 Unique Tower: "Kamikaze Airfield"

**Mechanic:** Builds aircraft over time. When enemies reach a critical mass, launches a kamikaze strike that crashes into the largest enemy cluster, dealing massive splash damage but consuming the stored planes.

**Playstyle:** Resource management - deciding when to unleash devastating strikes vs. saving for bigger threats.

**Strengths:**
- Enormous burst damage
- Great against clustered enemies
- Self-targeting based on threat

**Weaknesses:**
- Consumes aircraft resource
- Must rebuild after each use
- Wasted if enemies are spread out

3. **Nation-Specific Enemies** (Optional)
   - Currently all nations face the same Axis enemies
   - Could add nation-specific enemy variants later

### Balance Philosophy

Each nation should have:
- **Early game tower** (~$100-150) - Reliable DPS
- **Swarm control** (~$150-200) - Fast attack speed
- **Anti-armor specialist** (~$200-250) - High damage vs tanks
- **Area/splash damage** (~$250-300) - Mortar/artillery type
- **Long-range heavy** (~$300+) - Premium tower
- **Unique tower** - Nation-defining mechanic

### Design Goals

- **Distinct Playstyles:** Each nation should feel different to play
- **Historical Flavor:** Tower names and mechanics reflect WW2 reality
- **Strategic Variety:** Multiple viable strategies per nation
- **Balanced Power:** No nation dominates all situations
- **Unique Identity:** The unique tower defines each nation's character

### Design Template for Other Nations

When implementing other countries, use British stats as baseline and adjust for nation flavor:

- **US:** Slightly lower costs, good all-around stats (mass production)
- **Soviet:** Higher damage, slower fire rate (brutal efficiency)
- **Germany:** Higher costs, best stats (engineering excellence)
- **Italy:** Lower damage, faster fire rate (mobility focus)
- **Japan:** Moderate stats, unique mechanics (tactical variety)
